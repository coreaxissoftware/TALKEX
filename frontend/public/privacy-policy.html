Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

CoreAxis Software ("we", "our", "us") built the TalkEx app as a free application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use TalkEx.

1. Information We Collect

When you use TalkEx, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, username, phone number, and profile photo that you provide during registration.
  • Messages: Your messages are end-to-end encrypted. We cannot read or access the content of your messages.
  • Media: Photos, videos, and files you share are encrypted and stored securely.
  • Usage Data: Basic app usage data such as last seen time, online status, and app version.
  • Device Information: Device type and operating system for compatibility purposes.
🔒 End-to-End Encryption: All messages and calls in TalkEx are protected with end-to-end encryption (ECDH P-256 + AES-256-GCM). This means only you and the person you're communicating with can read or listen to them. Not even TalkEx can access your encrypted content.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the messaging service
  • To identify you and connect you with other users
  • To deliver your messages to the intended recipients
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To send service-related notifications

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Messages are encrypted end-to-end and cannot be read by us
  • Account data is stored securely on our servers
  • We use HTTPS for all data transmission
  • Encryption keys are generated and stored locally on your device

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not display advertisements in the app. Your data is never used for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted messages are permanently removed from our servers
  • Disappearing messages are automatically deleted after the set timer expires
  • View-once media is deleted after it has been viewed

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View your account information at any time
  • Update: Modify your profile, name, and phone number
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data

7. Children's Privacy

TalkEx is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

TalkEx uses the following third-party services:

  • iTunes Search API: For music browsing (only search queries are sent; no personal data)
  • OpenStreetMap: For location sharing maps (only when you choose to share location)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date.
